# Mutelet 1.0.0

Mutelet 1.0.0 marks the stable feature set introduced in 0.2.0: menu bar microphone mute, Toggle and Push to Talk shortcuts, configurable HUD and persistent status, and optional mute maintenance and screen-edge feedback.

Mutelet is now available free on the [Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6826858511) and as a signed, notarized Developer ID download from this release. App Store updates come through the App Store. To update the DMG version, quit Mutelet and replace it with the newer download.

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 14 or later. Microphone control depends on the writable mute or input-volume controls exposed by the device. Mutelet does not capture audio.

See the [installation and switching instructions](https://github.com/jsoizo/mutelet#download) before changing distribution channels.
